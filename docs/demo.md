# Local demo

Run `npm run dev:recorded` and open localhost:3000. All cultural results are CACHED at their original retrieval times. Sample profiles and supplied planning facts are demonstrations, not current-user inference or live venue verification.

## Gathering and revisions

1. Choose the six-person evening.
2. Resolve Moonlight to the offered 2016 film.
3. Supply cost, intended activity minutes and access for returned film/artist options. Unknowns remain needs-review until then.
4. Inspect the programme, separate participant signals and invitation.
5. Lower the budget. Infeasible choices disappear without rediscovery.
6. Change one participant's taste and inspect targeted refresh/staleness.
7. Edit invitation wording. No cultural discovery call is needed.

## Broader outcomes

1. Open the Tokyo sample and type “Build a 3-day itinerary in Tokyo.” Ten place references appear; missing planning facts are not invented.
2. For three displayed names, enter “For [exact name]: cost $5, duration 20 minutes, access confirmed.” These are synthetic demonstration inputs.
3. Under “Adjust the journey”, supply a ten-minute transfer allowance. Inspect day allocations, totals and evidence. The allowance is not a route lookup.
4. Type “Budget $4.” Previously selected stops become unaffordable.
5. Type “Create a cultural research report.” Inspect the claim ledger, caveats and exported evidence JSON.
6. In a new workspace, start with Radiohead and ask “Build a playlist with 120 minutes and budget $20.” Supply thirty-minute segments and $5 access for two returned artists. Type “Transition allowance: 5 minutes.” The artist programme totals sixty-five minutes.
7. On the Tokyo sample, use Connections to explore artists from Ghibli Museum. Ask “Develop a music direction from these artists.” The place remains a context anchor, not a personal taste or a restaurant experiment.
